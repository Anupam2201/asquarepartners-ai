The production website must recreate these approved surfaces as **dynamic HTML/CSS/JS/SVG/Canvas interactions**. Do not ship the frozen screenshots as the product UI. ## Shared visual and truth standards

- Premium dark PRISM palette with restrained cyan / teal / violet / amber accents.
- Large readable typography; avoid miniature labels as a design crutch.
- Meaningful graphics over decorative diagrams.
- Motion must communicate data flow, resolution, relationship discovery, investigation or measurement.
- Synthetic/example names and values must be clearly treated as illustration/demo data where applicable.
- Evidence, provenance, confidence and truth-state distinctions remain visible where relevant.
- AI recommendation must remain distinct from authorized human decision.
- “Alert closure” is not represented as the outcome.
- Avoid unsupported improvement percentages or ROI claims.
