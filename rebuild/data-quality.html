DATA QUALITY · EVIDENCE INTEGRITY · AI-NATIVE CONTROL

PRISM does not silently turn weak evidence into certainty.

Missing, stale, conflicting or unsourced data changes confidence, reasoning and action eligibility. Data quality is not a dashboard beside AI — it is part of what AI is allowed to believe and do.
